# Card 1 — Ethics & Institutional Integrity Philosophy

### Purpose of governance ethics

Governance ethics ensures Triton decisions under stress remain **principled, evidenced, and institution-first**—not convenient, political, or personally advantageous. Integrity is how the organization earns and keeps **audit trust, LP confidence, and internal escalation honesty**.

### Why Triton governance requires institutional ethics and decision integrity

| Without ethics discipline | With integrity discipline |
|---------------------------|---------------------------|
| Halt lifted for relationship pressure | Dual approval + minutes (93, 106) |
| Evidence trimmed before audit | 107 retention; 96 completeness |
| Executive “just resume” | 104 ratification boundaries |
| Incentive to hide Critical | 90 never conceal from exec line |
| Founder exception culture | 111 anti-dependency; duty over preference |
| Quiet quarter narrative | 110 earned readiness, not sympathy |

### Core principles

| Principle | Ethics meaning |
|-----------|----------------|
| **Capital Preservation Doctrine supremacy** | Integrity demands contain before optimize |
| **Integrity before convenience** | Shortcuts that weaken safeguards are rejected |
| **Constitutional safeguards dominate** | Ethics does not negotiate halts for optics |
| **Truth before comfort** | Report Critical; no spin in 96 |
| **Containment before ego** | Being right < being safe |
| **Institutional trust over short-term gain** | No hidden P&L pressure on governance |
| **Evidence before politics** | Artifacts decide; narratives support only |
| **Duty over personal preference** | Role duty per 93 exceeds individual will |

### What governance ethics proves

- Values are **named and operationalized** (Card 2)
- Ethical pressure scenarios have **classification and response** (Cards 3–5)
- Incentive and corruption risks are **actively countered** (Card 6)
- Trust reviews occur on **cadence** (Card 7)
- Power is exercised with **humility and duty** (Card 8)

### What governance ethics cannot guarantee

- Moral perfection of individuals
- Zero conflicts of interest in all forms
- Prevention of all bad-faith acts
- Legal or regulatory compliance by itself
- Market outcomes or profitability
- That ethical intent always prevents error

# Card 4 — Decision Integrity Operating Model

```
Identify pressure → Assess constitutional implications → Review evidence
→ Escalate if integrity risk → Contain incentive distortion
→ Document rationale → Review institutional impact
```

---

### Identify pressure

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Purpose** | Name ethical stress |
| **Required actions** | Open `GOVETH-*`; classify Card 3 |
| **What NOT to do** | Normalize pressure as “normal business” |
| **Escalation expectation** | Senior Op → Lead if operator receives pressure |
| **Evidence expectation** | Who, when, what was requested |

---

### Assess constitutional implications

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Purpose** | Safeguard impact |
| **Required actions** | Map 100, 93; flag if lift/override sought |
| **What NOT to do** | Trade safeguards for speed |
| **Escalation expectation** | Committee if constitutional |
| **Evidence expectation** | Safeguard checklist |

---

### Review evidence

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Purpose** | Truth before decision |
| **Required actions** | GCC, logs, `INC-*`; refuse decision on missing evidence |
| **What NOT to do** | Suppress or edit artifacts |
| **Escalation expectation** | GOVERNANCE_INTEGRITY_RISK if tampering suspected |
| **Evidence expectation** | Immutable retention per 107 |

---

### Escalate if integrity risk exists

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Purpose** | Remove solo judgment under pressure |
| **Required actions** | Escalate per 90/93; recuse conflicted party |
| **What NOT to do** | Handle executive pressure alone |
| **Escalation expectation** | Up-chain mandatory for EXECUTIVE_PRESSURE |
| **Evidence expectation** | ESC record |

---

### Contain incentive distortion

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Purpose** | Stop harm |
| **Required actions** | Halt/lock per 90; defer P&L narrative |
| **What NOT to do** | Resume to “fix the day” |
| **Escalation expectation** | Hard Halt → 106+104 path |
| **Evidence expectation** | Halt timestamp |

---

### Document rationale

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Purpose** | Audit and institutional memory |
| **Required actions** | Minutes, `GOVETH` closure memo, 96 if material |
| **What NOT to do** | Post-hoc rationalization without facts |
| **Escalation expectation** | Audit if external-facing |
| **Evidence expectation** | Decision rationale linked to artifacts |

### Review institutional impact

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Purpose** | Trust and readiness |

# Card 5 — Ethical Failure & Integrity Breach Playbook

| Scenario | What happened | Immediate containment | Escalation | Evidence | Recovery | Failure implication |
|----------|---------------|----------------------|------------|----------|----------|---------------------|
| **Incentive drift** | Performance narrative weakens halts | Default contain; document pressure | Committee | `GOVETH`, KPI | Incentive review; 114 hold | Repeat → Exec attestation |
| **Governance corruption risk** | Bribery, kickback, or quid pro quo suspected | Suspend approvals; preserve logs | Committee+Exec **24h**; counsel | All comms/logs | Investigation outcome documented | Termination paths external to this doc |
| **Hidden conflict of interest** | Undisclosed interest in outcome | Recuse; void decision if made | Committee **5bd** | Disclosure form | Alternate approver | Trust decay |
| **Evidence suppression** | Logs/reports omitted | Cease edits; snapshot 107 | GOVERNANCE_INTEGRITY_RISK | Forensic copy | Restore chain | Audit adverse |
| **Executive pressure override** | Lift without vote | Restore Hard Halt | Committee+Exec same day | `GOVCOMM`, attestation | Ratify or rollback | Founder exceptionalism |
| **Escalation concealment** | Severity minimized | Reclassify per 90 | Lead → Committee | `INC-*` history | Retrain 97 | SLA miss culture |
| **Governance dishonesty** | False attestation or report | Withhold cert 110 | Committee **48h** | 96, attestations | Corrective disclosure | LP/regulator risk |
| **Trust erosion** | Diligence failure or internal whistle | Transparent plan | Committee+Exec **5bd** | 107 pack | Remediation owners | Cert revoke |
| **Convenience over safeguards** | Skip dual approval | Block action | Lead immediate | 93 matrix | Discipline + drill 95 | CLPR breach |

# Card 6 — Incentive Alignment & Anti-Corruption Model

| Risk | Why dangerous | Failure consequence | Escalation | Corrective expectation |
|------|---------------|---------------------|------------|------------------------|
| **Incentive corruption** | Rewards oppose containment | Hidden risk-taking | Executive+Committee | Decouple bonus from unsafe lift |
| **Short-termism** | Quarter optics over integrity | Maturity fake (114) | Committee | Long-term KPI weighting in 92 review |
| **Ego-driven governance** | Personal legacy over institution | Bypass culture | Card 8 | Humility training; war game fail valued |
| **Personal-interest governance** | Self-dealing in decisions | Conflict | Recuse + `GOVETH` | SoD enforcement 93 |
| **Authority abuse** | Power without evidence | Override habit | Committee | Revoke cert; supervised period |
| **Institutional manipulation** | Spin, selective disclosure | Trust collapse | 107 adverse path | Full timeline disclosure |
| **Safeguard circumvention** | Technical or social workaround | Capital loss | Immediate Critical | 103 + 98 remediation |

**Anti-corruption rule:** No governance decision may be **conditioned on** undisclosed personal benefit. Gifts, favors, and related-party deals require **written disclosure** to Lead before any governance action on affected matter.
