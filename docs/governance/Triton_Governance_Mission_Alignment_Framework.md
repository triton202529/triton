## Scope disclaimer

This framework ensures Triton **governance stays aligned** with institutional mission, constitutional purpose, and long-term objectives across growth, stress, and decades—not business strategy execution, product roadmaps, or trading mandates.

> **Mission alignment preserves institutional direction — not guaranteed outcomes.**

**Alignment record ID:** `GOVALIGN-YYYY-MM-DD-###` — drift review, purpose conflict, or strategic coherence concern; links to `GOVETH-*`, `GOVCAP-*`, `GOVMETA-*`, `GOVINTEL-*`.

**Not strategic planning:** Business plans and execution targets live outside this manual; **governance** tests whether control-layer decisions serve stated institutional purpose.

**Documentation only.** No code, UI, broker, execution, or governance JSON mutation.

# Card 1 — Mission Integrity Philosophy

### Purpose of mission integrity governance

Mission integrity ensures every governance action—manual expansion, cert grant, lift vote, external claim—**serves** Triton’s constitutional purpose: capital preservation, institutional discipline, fiduciary responsibility, and durable trust—not short-term optics, opportunistic expansion, or narrative drift.

### Why institutions fail when mission, governance, and strategy drift apart

| Drift pattern | Alignment discipline |
|---------------|---------------------|
| “Grow at all costs” vs halts | 118 stewardship; Card 3 CRITICAL deviation |
| Governance library ≠ practice | 113 + 122 coherence |
| LP deck promises ≠ 110 readiness | 117 trust alignment |
| New manuals without maturity | 114 anti-complexity |
| Profit narrative overrides purpose | Card 5 profit-over-purpose |
| Founder vision ≠ Step 100 | 100 supremacy; `GOVALIGN` |

### Core principles

| Principle | Mission meaning |
|-----------|-----------------|
| **Capital Preservation Doctrine supremacy** | Purpose anchor—never subordinated |
| **Purpose before convenience** | Alignment check before expedient bypass |
| **Mission before expansion** | Scope growth requires purpose fit |
| **Constitutional safeguards dominate** | Purpose never waives safeguards |
| **Stewardship over opportunism** | 118 fiduciary lens |
| **Strategic coherence** | Decisions consistent across library |
| **Institutional consistency** | 121 precedent + 113 priority |
| **Long-term responsibility** | 114/115 horizon over quarter |

### What mission alignment proves

- **Ten purpose domains** monitored (Card 2)
- Drift is **classified** and escalated (Card 3)
- Material decisions pass **alignment loop** (Card 4)
- Mission conflicts have **playbooks** (Card 5)
- Annual **purpose review** occurs (Card 7)

### What mission alignment cannot guarantee

- Market outperformance or fundraising success
- Perfect agreement on all strategic tradeoffs
- Immunity from external pressure
- That aligned governance prevents losses
- Replacement of executive business judgment on non-governance matters
