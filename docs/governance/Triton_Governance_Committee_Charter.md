## Charter statement

The **Governance Committee** is the institutional body that adjudicates **constitutional and material governance decisions** for Triton: policy exceptions, Hard Halt lift (with Executive), constitutional safeguard changes, maturity attestation, and governance manual evolution (Step 98).

> **The Governance Committee governs constitutional decisions — not discretionary trading execution.**

This charter is **procedural**. It does not implement voting systems, mutate runtime, or enable execution.

**Capital Preservation Doctrine** governs all Committee deliberations: when evidence is incomplete, **default to containment and rejection of relaxation**.

**Committee record ID:** `GOVCOMM-YYYY-MM-DD-###` | Link to `GOVCHG-*`, `INC-*`, minutes.

# Card 1 — Governance Committee Philosophy

### Purpose of the Governance Committee

The Committee exists to provide **documented, quorum-based, evidence-first adjudication** where operator or Lead authority is insufficient: constitutional pressure, Level 4 governance, Hard Halt lift, material policy change, safeguard evolution, and institutional attestation (maturity/readiness).

It prevents **single-point governance failure** and **personality-driven policy**.

### Why the Governance Committee exists

| Need | Committee function |
|------|-------------------|
| Constitutional boundaries | Vote on exceptions and safeguard changes |
| Capital protection at scale | Ratify Hard Halt lift with Executive |
| Institutional memory | Minutes, dissent, prevention owners |
| Audit and oversight | Qualify or adverse-find governance packs |
| Separation from operations | Committee does not run GCC shifts |

### Core principles

| Principle | Committee meaning |
|-----------|-------------------|
| **Governance before execution** | No Committee action implies trade authorization |
| **Capital Preservation Doctrine supremacy** | Reject relaxation without evidence |
| **Constitutional safeguards dominate** | Unanimous quorum for highest tier (Step 98) |
| **Evidence-first adjudication** | No vote on narrative-only packages |
| **Escalation before intervention** | Receive via Lead chain—not informal lobby |
| **Containment-first governance** | Default deny on lift and override |
| **Institutional discipline over personality** | Votes recorded; dissent preserved |
| **Stability over emotion** | Defer votes if emotional urgency without package |

### What the committee is responsible for

- Voting on material governance policy and **Step 98** changes (medium–critical tier)
- Hard Halt **lift** participation with Risk / Governance Lead validation + **Executive ratification**
- Constitutional and autonomy-boundary incidents (Step 90 Level 4 adjunct)
- Override paths requiring Committee per [Step 93](./Triton_Governance_Roles_Authority_Framework.md) (lifecycle freeze, governance lock, constitutional override)
- Maturity promotion to **INSTITUTIONAL_GRADE** and readiness **grant/withhold** review (Step 94)
- Regression triggers and **GOVERNANCE_DRIFT** / safeguard weakening response
- Failed governance test **Critical** remediation oversight (Step 95)
- Quarterly audit pack **qualification** (Step 96)
- Convening within **24 hours** for active Level 4 / CRITICAL GWS

### What the committee is NOT responsible for

- Routine operator monitoring or Step 102 playbook commands
- Level 1–2 incident closure (Operator / Senior Operator)
- Level 3 day-to-day ownership (Risk / Governance Lead)—Committee on escalation only
- Technical pipeline repair (System Administrator)
- Trading, allocation, or broker strategy
- Implementing code or editing governance JSON in production
- Replacing Executive ratification on constitutional tier

Aligned with [Step 93](./Triton_Governance_Roles_Authority_Framework.md), [Step 94](./Triton_Governance_Lifecycle_Maturity_Framework.md), [Step 98](./Triton_Governance_Change_Management_Framework.md), [Step 100](./Triton_Governance_Constitution_Operating_Charter.md).

|----------------|----------------------|--------------------|-------------------|------------|----------------------|

| **Executive escalation routing** | Convene Executive for L4; co-sign attestation | Chair initiates | Active Critical criteria | **15 min** L4 notify verify | Bypassing Lead |

# Card 5 — Committee Approval Framework

Aligned with [Step 98](./Triton_Governance_Change_Management_Framework.md).

| Approval type | Who proposes | Who reviews | Who approves | Dual approval? | Executive? | Evidence | Rollback |
|---------------|--------------|-------------|--------------|----------------|------------|----------|----------|
| **Routine governance approval** | Governance Lead | Committee observer | Lead (patch/minor ack) | No | No | Diff, `GOVCHG` | Patch discretionary |
| **Emergency governance approval** | Lead + Chair | ≥2 Committee | Chair + Executive | Yes | **Yes** | Active `INC-*`; sunset ≤72h | Mandatory at sunset |
| **Constitutional safeguard revision** | Committee member / Lead | Full Committee | **Unanimous quorum** + Executive | Yes | **Yes** | Impact memo, 95 pass | Pre-authorized emergency |
| **Maturity promotion (INSTITUTIONAL)** | Lead | Committee | **Quorum vote** | N/A | Executive ack | 94 gate pack | Regression trigger |
| **Override exception (Committee tier)** | Risk Lead | Committee | Quorum + dual approvers per 93 | **Yes** | If constitutional path | Step 96 override report | Expiration + post-review |
| **Governance rollback approval** | Lead / Committee | Same or higher tier | Per 98: Committee High/Critical | Yes if original High+ | Executive if constitutional | Rollback `GOVCHG` | Execute immediately |

**Vote discipline:** Quorum = majority of seated members unless Step 98 specifies unanimous. Abstentions recorded. No proxy votes without written delegation on file.

# Card 7 — Committee Decision Workflows

---

### Hard Halt decision

```
Escalation (L4 chain) → Evidence review → Committee review → Vote
→ Executive ratification → Documentation → Monitoring (2 cycles + post-incident)
```

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Purpose** | Restore execution only when integrity proven |
| **Why sequence matters** | Executive without Committee (or reverse) violates Step 93 |
| **Escalation expectation** | Incomplete Phase 6 → reject lift |
| **Failure prevention** | Pre-drafted rollback to halt if regression |

---

### Governance change approval

```
Proposal (GOVCHG) → Committee review → Evidence validation (98 tier)
→ Approve / reject → Rollback readiness → Effective date (after drill if High+)
→ Version register → Training bulletin (97)
```

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Purpose** | Safe manual evolution |
| **Why sequence matters** | Effective before drill causes drift |
| **Escalation expectation** | Constitutional → unanimous + Executive |
| **Failure prevention** | Rollback version identified pre-vote |

---

### Override exception review

```
Request logged → Constitutional relevance check → Evidence review (dual approvers named)
→ Approve / deny → Documentation (96) → Expiration enforced → Post-override review scheduled
```

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Purpose** | Exception-only discipline |
| **Why sequence matters** | Approval before dual naming = invalid |
| **Escalation expectation** | Deny constitutional path without Executive path |
| **Failure prevention** | Freeze if OVERRIDE_DEPENDENCY active |

# Card 9 — Committee Operating Checklist

**Per scheduled session** (weekly minimum; **immediate** session if CRITICAL / Hard Halt).

### Health and posture

- [ ] Governance Health Score and GWS reviewed
- [ ] Open Critical KPIs = **none** or on agenda with owner
- [ ] Hard Halt status: **NONE** or lift on agenda with full package

### Queues and trends

- [ ] Escalation queue: open L3+ reviewed with Lead
- [ ] Override trend (OF) and dependency flags
- [ ] Constitutional safeguards: CLPR / violations **NONE**

### Institutional items

- [ ] Governance deterioration signals and regression triggers
- [ ] Readiness / maturity items: no grant with blockers R1–R8
- [ ] Failed governance tests (Critical/Material) on remediation tracker
- [ ] Pending `GOVCHG` votes: evidence complete per tier

### Session close

- [ ] All votes recorded in `GOVCOMM-*` minutes
- [ ] Executive notified for constitutional outcomes same day
- [ ] Prevention owners and due dates assigned
- [ ] Next session scheduled
