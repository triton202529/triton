## Scope disclaimer

This framework governs **how Triton's governance constitution may be amended, expanded, restricted, retired, or protected** over decades—constitutional preservation doctrine and change-authority discipline. It **complements** Step 98 (`GOVCHG` execution), Step 100 (supreme charter), and Step 112 (`GOVMETA` self-improvement loop). Step 128 defines **what may change at what protection level**; Step 98 implements approved changes.

> **Constitutional evolution preserves institutional relevance — not guaranteed outcomes.**

**Constitutional amendment record ID:** `GOVAMEND-YYYY-MM-DD-###` — proposal, classification, ratification, or retirement; approved amendments execute via `GOVCHG-*` per Step 98; links to `GOVPREC-*`, `GOVALIGN-*`, `GOVCOMM-*`.

**Not legal implementation:** This is internal governance doctrine—not corporate charter filing, regulatory submission, or organizational restructuring.

**Documentation only.** No code, UI, broker, execution, or governance JSON mutation.

# Card 1 — Constitutional Evolution Philosophy

### Purpose of constitutional amendment governance

Constitutional amendment governance ensures Triton's supreme governance layer **adapts without eroding**—relevance over decades while Capital Preservation Doctrine, safeguard discipline, and audit defensibility remain intact.

### Why governance systems require controlled evolution without allowing constitutional erosion

| Ungoverned evolution risk | Preservation outcome |
|---------------------------|----------------------|
| Oral “constitutional” edits | Written `GOVAMEND` + `GOVCHG` only |
| Safeguard loosening under pressure | HIGH_PROTECTION tier lock |
| Manual sprawl without retirement | RETIRABLE sunset discipline |
| Founder rewrite without Committee | STRUCTURAL requires quorum + Executive |
| Emergency becomes permanent amendment | Exception sunset mandatory |
| Amendment without mission check | 124 `GOVALIGN` gate |
| Churn without evidence | Evidence gate per Card 3 |

### Core principles

| Principle | Constitutional meaning |
|-----------|------------------------|
| **Capital Preservation Doctrine supremacy** | No amendment trades containment for convenience |
| **Preservation before modification** | Default: retain; change requires justification |
| **Constitutional integrity before convenience** | Stricter interpretation during transition (98) |
| **Governance evolution with restraint** | Prefer clarify over expand (112) |
| **Mission continuity** | 124 alignment before ratification |
| **Evidence-based amendment** | Weakness or gap must be evidenced |
| **Institutional durability** | Decades-scale record preservation |
| **Controlled adaptation** | Classified path only (Card 3) |

### What constitutional evolution proves

- Every governance component maps to a **protection category** (Card 2)
- Every amendment proposal maps to an **amendment class** (Card 3)
- Material and constitutional changes follow the **preservation-first loop** (Card 4)
- Failures trigger **playbooks** (Card 5) before drift compounds
- **Annual constitutional integrity review** occurs (Card 7)
- Approved changes enter **versioned constitutional record** (98 register)

### What constitutional evolution cannot guarantee

- Perfect governance for all future conditions
- Zero incidents after amendment
- That every proposal will be approved
- Automatic improvement in GHS, IRS, or maturity scores
- Permission to relax live runtime controls via documentation
- Immunity from external legal or regulatory override

# Card 3 — Constitutional Change Classification Model

Six amendment classes. Every `GOVAMEND` tags one class before Committee review. Execution tier maps to Step 98.

---

### CLARIFICATION_CHANGE

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Definition** | Grammar, cross-links, examples—no authority, threshold, or safeguard change |
| **Approval expectation** | Governance Lead; Committee notification |
| **Escalation expectation** | None unless operator confusion |
| **Evidence requirement** | Diff; rationale that meaning unchanged |
| **Review expectation** | Patch version in 98 register |
| **Failure implication** | Hidden policy shift if misclassified |
| **Revocation expectation** | Revert patch if confusion reported **24h** |

---

### MINOR_GOVERNANCE_CHANGE

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Definition** | Operational rule within existing authority; no safeguard or mission touch |
| **Approval expectation** | Governance Lead; Senior Operator review if operator-facing |
| **Escalation expectation** | Lead if trading-window impact |
| **Evidence requirement** | Rationale; affected manuals listed |
| **Review expectation** | Minor version bump; drill if procedure change |
| **Failure implication** | Drift if bypassed |
| **Revocation expectation** | Rollback **5bd** if drill fail (98) |

---

### MATERIAL_GOVERNANCE_CHANGE

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Definition** | Alters approval hierarchy, KPI thresholds, committee process, or multi-manual coherence |
| **Approval expectation** | Committee quorum vote |
| **Escalation expectation** | Executive if external-facing or cert impact |
| **Evidence requirement** | Incident, audit, KPI, or drill evidence |
| **Review expectation** | Training refresh (97); readiness hold until pass (94) |
| **Failure implication** | Oral governance; readiness withheld |
| **Revocation expectation** | Committee vote to revert |

---

### CONSTITUTIONAL_AMENDMENT

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Definition** | Alters HIGH_PROTECTION provision, Step 100 charter text, or non-delegable boundary (127) |
| **Approval expectation** | Committee quorum + Executive signature |
| **Escalation expectation** | Mandatory—no Lead-only path |
| **Evidence requirement** | Multi-source evidence; impact assessment; rollback draft |
| **Review expectation** | Annual ack; `GOVPREC` if novel interpretation |
| **Failure implication** | Constitutional erosion |
| **Revocation expectation** | Same tier to revert; emergency rollback per 98 |

---

### STRUCTURAL_CONSTITUTIONAL_CHANGE

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Definition** | Reorganizes constitutional architecture: new supreme tier, manual hierarchy change, permanent safeguard removal |
| **Approval expectation** | Committee supermajority + Executive; external counsel review if requested |
| **Escalation implication** | **Mandatory** Committee session; 115 scenario review if systemic |
| **Evidence requirement** | Full impact pack; mission alignment (124); succession impact (111) |
| **Review expectation** | Semi-annual structural fitness review |
| **Failure implication** | Institutional instability; trust loss |
| **Revocation expectation** | Same supermajority; interim stricter rule until resolved |

---

### CONSTITUTIONAL_EXCEPTION_REQUEST

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Definition** | Time-bound waiver of STANDARD or OPERATIONAL rule—not HIGH_PROTECTION or PERMANENT principle |
| **Approval expectation** | Committee+Executive if touches MATERIAL; Lead+Committee if operational only |
| **Escalation expectation** | Up-tier if safeguard adjacent |
| **Evidence requirement** | Scoped waiver; sunset date; monitoring plan |
| **Review expectation** | Mandatory expiry review; no renewal without new evidence |
| **Failure implication** | Shadow constitution via permanent exception |
| **Revocation expectation** | Auto-expire at sunset; early revoke on misuse |

# Card 4 — Constitutional Change Operating Model

```
Identify change proposal → Assess constitutional category → Review mission implications
→ Review governance implications → Escalate if required → Document rationale
→ Committee review → Approve, reject, or revise → Preserve constitutional record
```

---

### Identify change proposal

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Purpose** | Formalize intent before edit |
| **Required actions** | Open `GOVAMEND`; name proposer; cite trigger (incident, audit, `GOVMETA`, maturity gap) |
| **What NOT to do** | Edit manual before record opened |
| **Escalation expectation** | Lead triage **48h** |
| **Evidence expectation** | Proposal summary |

---

### Assess constitutional category

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Purpose** | Map to Card 2 protection level |
| **Required actions** | Tag component category; default stricter if ambiguous |
| **What NOT to do** | Downgrade protection to ease approval |
| **Escalation expectation** | PERMANENT/HIGH touch → CONSTITUTIONAL_AMENDMENT class |
| **Evidence expectation** | Category tag in `GOVAMEND` |

---

### Review mission implications

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Purpose** | Prevent mission drift via amendment (124) |
| **Required actions** | `GOVALIGN` check; purpose conflict scan |
| **What NOT to do** | Approve growth-driven weakening |
| **Escalation expectation** | CRITICAL_MISSION_DEVIATION → reject or revise |
| **Evidence expectation** | Alignment memo |

---

### Review governance implications

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Purpose** | Cross-manual coherence (113) |
| **Required actions** | List affected steps; SoD check (93); delegation impact (127) |
| **What NOT to do** | Single-manual edit ignoring dependencies |
| **Escalation expectation** | Committee if multi-manual MATERIAL+ |
| **Evidence expectation** | Impact matrix |

---

### Escalate if required

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Purpose** | No ultra vires amendment path |
| **Required actions** | Apply Card 3 class; invoke 90 chain if urgency |
| **What NOT to do** | Emergency oral amendment |
| **Escalation expectation** | Per class—CONSTITUTIONAL+ always Committee+Executive |
| **Evidence expectation** | ESC record if incident-linked |

---

### Document rationale

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Purpose** | Audit-grade why |
| **Required actions** | Evidence pack; alternatives considered; rollback draft |
| **What NOT to do** | “Best practice” without Triton evidence |
| **Escalation expectation** | Weak evidence → return to proposer |
| **Evidence expectation** | Rationale attachment |

---

### Committee review

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Purpose** | Institutional ratification |
| **Required actions** | Quorum per 106; vote recorded; dissent noted |
| **What NOT to do** | Ratify without reading HIGH_PROTECTION impact |
| **Escalation expectation** | Deadlock → Executive mediation; no default approve |
| **Evidence expectation** | `GOVCOMM-*` minutes |

---

### Approve, reject, or revise

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Purpose** | Decision with accountability |
| **Required actions** | Approve → assign `GOVCHG`; reject → archive with reason; revise → return with conditions |
| **What NOT to do** | Partial oral approve |
| **Escalation expectation** | STRUCTURAL → supermajority |
| **Evidence expectation** | Signed decision record |

---

### Preserve constitutional record

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Purpose** | Decades-scale memory |
| **Required actions** | Update 98 version register; effective date; training flag; link `GOVPREC` if precedent-setting |
| **What NOT to do** | Effective date before training if MATERIAL+ |
| **Escalation expectation** | Record failure → Card 5 |
| **Evidence expectation** | `GOVVER-*` entry; archived prior version |

# Card 5 — Constitutional Failure Playbooks

| Scenario | What happened | Immediate containment | Escalation | Evidence | Recovery | Constitutional implication |
|----------|---------------|----------------------|------------|----------|----------|---------------------------|
| **Unauthorized amendment** | Manual edited without `GOVAMEND`/`GOVCHG` | Revert to last registered version; stricter ops | Committee **48h** | Diff; who edited | Discipline path external; re-train | Ultra vires governance |
| **Constitutional drift** | Practice ≠ written constitution | Operate stricter rule (100, 113) | Lead **24h** | Gap log | Formalize via 98 or correct practice | Erosion risk |
| **Mission-conflicting amendment** | Change weakens purpose | Suspend effective date | Committee+Exec | `GOVALIGN` | Reject or narrow scope | 124 breach |
| **Emergency amendment pressure** | “Change now” without process | Hold posture; no runtime relax | Executive **24h** ratify or reject | Pressure log | Exception path only with sunset | Emergency ≠ structural |
| **Governance weakening proposal** | Loosen safeguard without evidence | Block; require evidence pack | Committee | Proposal + counter-evidence | Reject or tighten alternative | HIGH_PROTECTION threat |
| **Constitutional ambiguity** | Two readings of charter | Stricter containment (113) | Committee **5bd** | Conflicting citations | `GOVPREC` or CLARIFICATION | Interpretation gap |
| **Repeated amendment churn** | Same topic re-amended quarterly | Moratorium **90d** unless incident | Committee | Churn log | Root-cause via 119/112 | Instability |
| **Amendment without evidence** | Opinion-driven change | Return to proposer | Lead | Missing evidence list | Resubmit with evidence | Invalid proposal |
| **Constitutional record failure** | Version register out of sync | Freeze MATERIAL+ effective dates | Lead + Audit **24h** | Register audit | Reconcile 98 register | Audit defensibility loss |

# Card 12 — Constitutional Amendment Appendix

### Step hierarchy (amendment authority)

| Step | Role in constitutional evolution |
|------|----------------------------------|
| **100** | Supreme charter—PERMANENT principles |

### Glossary

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Amendment classification** | Card 3 tier (CLARIFICATION through EXCEPTION) |
