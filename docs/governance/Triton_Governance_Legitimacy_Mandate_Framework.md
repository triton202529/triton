## Scope disclaimer

This framework governs **why Triton has the right to govern** and **how that legitimacy is continuously earned, renewed, validated, and preserved**—mandate discipline and social-license stewardship. It **complements** Step 117 (external stakeholder trust and evidence-aligned communication); Step 129 covers **governance acceptance doctrine**, mandate renewal, and authority justification over decades.

> **Legitimacy strengthens governance acceptance and trust — not guaranteed outcomes.**

**Mandate record ID:** `GOVMAND-YYYY-MM-DD-###` — legitimacy review, mandate renewal assessment, social-license concern, or mandate impairment; links to `GOVTRUST-*` (117), `GOVALIGN-*` (124), `GOVCOMM-*` (106).

# Card 1 — Legitimacy & Mandate Philosophy

### Purpose of governance legitimacy

Governance legitimacy is the **institutional right to exercise authority**—earned through disciplined stewardship, constitutional fidelity, fiduciary care, and continuous validation—not inherited from title, tenure, or self-declaration.

### Why authority that is merely inherited, assumed, or self-declared eventually fails

| Failure pattern | Mandate discipline |
|-----------------|-------------------|
| “We’ve always done it this way” | Annual mandate reassessment (Card 7) |
| Founder title = unlimited authority | 127 non-delegable boundaries; 111 succession |
| Performance narrative replaces evidence | 117 evidence alignment; 110 earned cert |
| Stakeholders excluded from accountability | 96 reporting; 107 diligence |
| Mission drift without renewal | 124 `GOVALIGN` gate |
| Crisis commands without ratification | 108 sunset; Committee ratify |
| Social license treated as permanent | Card 4 continuous renewal loop |

### Core principles

| Principle | Legitimacy meaning |
|-----------|-------------------|
| **Capital Preservation Doctrine supremacy** | Legitimacy follows containment—not bravado |
| **Legitimacy before authority** | Mandate must precede expanded scope |
| **Trust before control** | Acceptance enables discipline; control alone does not |
| **Stewardship before entitlement** | Authority is held in trust, not owned |
| **Constitutional safeguards dominate** | Legitimacy never buys safeguard waiver |
| **Earned authority** | Certification, evidence, and review—not assumption |
| **Continuous validation** | Mandate renewed, not permanent |
| **Institutional responsibility** | Named accountability for mandate health |

### What legitimacy proves

- **Twelve legitimacy domains** have defined signals and erosion markers (Card 2)
- Mandate condition maps to a **legitimacy state** (Card 3)
- Renewal follows a **stewardship-first loop** (Card 4)
- Legitimacy failures trigger **playbooks** (Card 5) before crisis
- **Annual mandate reassessment** occurs (Card 7)
- Social license is **monitored**, not assumed

### What legitimacy cannot guarantee

- Universal stakeholder approval
- Positive public sentiment or media coverage
- LP retention, fundraising success, or regulatory favor
- Immunity from challenge or scrutiny
- Market performance or operational perfection
- That authority will never be questioned

# Card 3 — Legitimacy Classification Model

Five states. Every `GOVMAND` assessment tags one state. Default stricter when indicators conflict.

---

### STRONG_LEGITIMACY

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Definition** | All domains green; mandate recently validated |
| **Observed indicators** | IRS pass; cert current; no open trust events; alignment ALIGNED |
| **Escalation expectation** | None routine |
| **Containment expectation** | Maintain discipline—no complacency |
| **Review expectation** | Quarterly spot-check |
| **Failure implication** | N/A—baseline to preserve |

---

### STABLE_LEGITIMACY

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Definition** | Minor gaps; no material stakeholder challenge |
| **Observed indicators** | Isolated SLA miss remediated; single-domain yellow in 122 |
| **Escalation expectation** | Lead owns remediation plan |
| **Containment expectation** | Close gaps before expansion (126) |
| **Review expectation** | Monthly until green |
| **Failure implication** | Drift if ignored |

---

### QUESTIONED_LEGITIMACY

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Definition** | Stakeholder or internal challenge to governance right or performance |
| **Observed indicators** | Diligence concern; operator petition; audit finding open |
| **Escalation expectation** | Committee **5bd** |
| **Containment expectation** | Evidence response; no narrative spin (117) |
| **Review expectation** | Weekly until STABLE |
| **Failure implication** | Mandate narrowing if unresolved |

---

### DEGRADED_LEGITIMACY

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Definition** | Multiple domains impaired; credibility materially at risk |
| **Observed indicators** | Cert at risk; repeat trust events; mission yellow |
| **Escalation expectation** | Committee+Executive **48h** |
| **Containment expectation** | Halt expansion; strengthen stewardship (Card 4) |
| **Review expectation** | Weekly Committee agenda item |
| **Failure implication** | Readiness withhold (110); scale freeze (126) |

---

### LEGITIMACY_CRISIS

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|

# Card 5 — Legitimacy Failure Playbooks

| Scenario | What happened | Immediate containment | Escalation | Evidence | Recovery | Legitimacy implication |
|----------|---------------|----------------------|------------|----------|----------|------------------------|
| **Trust erosion** | Stakeholder confidence declining | Evidence-aligned response (117); no spin | Committee **5bd** | Trust log; audit pack | Remediation plan | STABLE → QUESTIONED |
| **Stakeholder rejection** | Material withdrawal of confidence | Contain; transparent facts | Executive+Committee **24h** | Rejection record | Mandate review | LEGITIMACY_CRISIS risk |
| **Governance credibility loss** | “Governance doesn’t work” narrative | Demonstrate discipline; fix gaps | Lead **48h**; Committee if pattern | GHS/IRS; incident log | Visible remediation | Governance domain fail |
| **Leadership legitimacy concerns** | Challenge to leader mandate | Verify `GOVDELEG`; no ultra vires | `GOVSUCC` | Delegation roster | Re-document mandate | Leadership domain fail |
| **Fiduciary trust breakdown** | Capital stewardship challenged | Hard Halt if warranted; `GOVCAP` | Committee+Exec | Reconciliation; halt log | 118 remediation | Fiduciary domain fail |
| **Constitutional legitimacy challenge** | Charter authority disputed | Stricter rule (113); no oral fix | Committee+Exec **48h** | Challenge record | 128 clarify or defend | Constitutional domain fail |
| **Mission legitimacy concern** | Purpose no longer accepted | `GOVALIGN` review | Committee | Purpose memo | Realign or narrow scope | Mission domain fail |
| **Crisis legitimacy failure** | Emergency authority unratified | Revoke emergency; restore chain | Committee **24h** | Crisis log | Post-crisis mandate review | Crisis domain fail |
| **Social-license deterioration** | Acceptance to operate eroding | Stakeholder map; evidence plan | `GOVMAND` + `GOVTRUST` | License indicators | Renewal program (Card 4) | Social license fail |
