## Scope disclaimer

This framework governs **how Triton governance anticipates plausible futures**—scenario planning, weak-signal monitoring, and future-state readiness—without claiming predictive certainty. It is **not** forecasting engines, market models, runtime mutation, or authorization to trade on scenarios.

> **Governance foresight improves preparedness — not guaranteed prediction accuracy.**

**Foresight record ID:** `GOVFORE-YYYY-MM-DD-###` — scenario register entry; links to `GOVWAR-*` (109), `GOVREADY-*` (110), `GOVMETA-*` (112).

**Rehearsal rule:** Foresight scenarios are **tested** via Step 109 war games and Step 95 drills—never used to relax safeguards “because we predicted calm.”

**Documentation only.** No code, UI, broker, execution, or governance JSON mutation.

# Card 1 — Strategic Foresight Philosophy

### Purpose of governance foresight

Strategic foresight ensures Triton **prepares institutional responses** before regime shifts, structural stress, or converging crises force improvised governance. It converts uncertainty into **disciplined scenarios**, monitored signals, and rehearsed playbooks—not prophecies.

### Why governance must anticipate rather than merely react

| Reactive-only risk | Anticipatory outcome |
|--------------------|----------------------|
| First occurrence = design test | Scenarios pre-modeled (Card 2) |
| Quiet quarter = false safety | Weak signals monitored (Card 6) |
| Black swan = panic | Preparedness playbook (Card 5) |
| Scale surprise = key-person failure | Succession futures in 111 |
| Regulatory shock = evidence scramble | 107 retention ready |
| AI/tool change = safeguard gap | Developer boundaries rehearsed (103) |

### Core principles

| Principle | Foresight meaning |
|-----------|-------------------|
| **Capital Preservation Doctrine supremacy** | Scenarios default to contain-first |
| **Anticipation before reaction** | `GOVFORE` before crisis if signals warrant |
| **Resilience before certainty** | Prepare; do not predict |
| **Constitutional safeguards dominate** | Foresight never pre-authorizes loosening |
| **Humility toward unknown futures** | Card 8 anti-prediction discipline |
| **Evidence-first preparation** | War-game and audit artifacts |
| **Preparedness over prediction** | Readiness ≠ forecast accuracy |
| **Continuity under uncertainty** | 111 + 113 stable doctrine |

### What governance foresight proves

- Material futures are **named, classified, and owned** (Cards 2–3)
- Scenarios are **war-gamed** on cadence tied to horizon (Cards 4, 7)
- Weak signals feed **early warning** (99) without alarmism
- Committee reviews **future-state readiness** annually minimum
- Black swan paths **rehearsed**—not assumed impossible

### What governance foresight cannot guarantee

- Correct prediction of timing or magnitude
- Prevention of all tail events
- Market or regulatory outcomes
- That scenarios cover every unknown unknown
- Runtime expansion or trading authorization
- Immunity from institutional trust collapse

# Card 3 — Foresight Classification Model

| Class | Code | Definition | Time horizon | Risk implication | Governance expectation | Escalation | Failure implication |
|-------|------|------------|--------------|------------------|------------------------|------------|---------------------|
| **Near horizon** | NEAR | Plausible within **12 months** | 0–12 mo | Operational/tactical | Update 91/102 playbooks; quarterly signal review | Lead | Surprise if unmonitored |
| **Mid horizon** | MID | Structural pressure **1–3 years** | 12–36 mo | Process and capacity | 114 stage planning; training scale | Committee annual | Stage misalignment |
| **Long horizon** | LONG | Institutional **3–10 years** | 36–120 mo | Doctrine and continuity | 111 generational; 100 review | Committee+Exec attestation | Complacency |
| **Structural shift** | STRUCTURAL | Regime change in rules, scale, or tech | Variable | Constitutional or MATERIAL | 98 + 112 path before effective | Committee+Exec | Safeguard erosion |
| **Black swan preparedness** | BLACK_SWAN | Low probability, extreme impact | Any | Existential trust/capital | 109 extreme tier; 108 default contain | Immediate Committee+Exec on live event | Improvisation |

**Classification rule:** When uncertain, classify **up** one horizon and **contain** until Committee affirms downgrade.

→ Monitor signals → Reassess readiness

# Card 5 — Black Swan & Extreme Future Playbook

| Scenario | What it means | Preparedness expectation | Escalation | Evidence required | Recovery implication | What NOT to assume |
|----------|---------------|--------------------------|------------|-------------------|----------------------|-------------------|
| **Systemic market collapse** | Liquidity/correlation crisis | Hard Halt discipline; no lift for convenience | 108 systemic; Committee+Exec | Halt log; `GOVCRISIS-*` | Normalization per 108 only | Models predict floor |
| **Governance + market crisis** | Dual stress | Crisis cell; defer 98/112 changes | Committee+Exec continuous | Convergence war-game record | 90d elevated monitoring | Trading team alone decides |
| **Geopolitical instability** | Macro + ops disruption | 109 convergence; comms in 96 | Lead daily; Exec if CRITICAL | Brief archive | Extend monitoring | “Outside our scope” |
| **AI governance disruption** | Tool/model breaks SoD or evidence | 103 boundaries; observe-only default | Lead; Committee if MATERIAL | Change log; no JSON mutation | `GOVMETA` remediation | AI output = approval |
| **Regulatory restructuring** | New rules or examiner focus | 107 retention; counsel path | Committee **10bd** | Audit pack draft | `GOVCHG` updates | Oral compliance |
| **Organizational hypergrowth** | Rapid scale | 114 gates; cert surge 97 | Committee capability review | Roster; SLA metrics | Slow complexity add | Maturity label = ready |
| **Founder transition under crisis** | 111 + 108 overlap | `GOVSUCC` + delegate list live | FOUNDER_ABSENCE + crisis tier | Delegate attestation | Ratify backlog 5bd | Founder reachable informally |
| **Institutional trust collapse** | LP/audit/regulator loss of confidence | 107 adverse playbook; no spin | Committee+Exec **5bd** | Diligence Q&A index | Transparent remediation | Hide Critical from exec line |
| **Multi-crisis convergence** | 3+ domains Card 2 active | 109 Tier 4; suspend promotion 110/114 | Committee crisis cell | Open scenario register | 90d no stage advance | Prioritize one silo |

**Black swan discipline:** Preparedness = **containment + evidence + rehearsed authority**—not scenario betting or leverage increase.

| **Governance complacency** | SOVEREIGN stage arrogance | Regression | 114 annual attestation | Stage hold |
