# Card 1 — Audit & Readiness Philosophy

### Purpose of governance audit readiness

Audit readiness ensures Triton can **demonstrate** that governance controls were **defined, followed, evidenced, and overseen** over a review period—so investors, LPs, boards, and auditors can rely on **process integrity**, not narrative alone.

### Why Triton governance must be institutionally defensible

| Stakeholder need | Defensibility mechanism |
|------------------|-------------------------|
| **Capital providers** | Halts, overrides, and incidents are traceable |
| **Internal / external audit** | ACR, retention, committee minutes |
| **Regulator-style inquiry** | Segregation of duties, CLPR, no silent mutation |
| **Institutional continuity** | Manuals versioned (Step 98); training certs (Step 97) |
| **Executive accountability** | Scorecards and attestation (Steps 96, 100, 104) |

### Core principles

| Principle | Audit meaning |
|-----------|---------------|
| **Evidence-first governance** | Claims cite `INC-*`, `GOVRPT-*`, `GOVCOMM-*`, artifacts |
| **Capital Preservation Doctrine supremacy** | Containment decisions defensible under stress |
| **Auditability before trust** | Trust follows samples, not slogans |
| **Constitutional safeguards dominate** | Weakest link is highlighted, not hidden |
| **Institutional transparency** | Qualified findings disclosed with remediation |
| **Traceability** | UTC, IDs, approver roles end-to-end |
| **Escalation defensibility** | Chain and SLA demonstrable |
| **Containment-first governance** | Resume requires documented approval path |

### What audit readiness proves

- Governance manuals (Steps 90–106) existed and were version-controlled
- KPIs, incidents, halts, overrides, and committee actions were **logged** per SOP
- Segregation of duties was **designed** (Step 93) and **sampled** (Step 97 OCR)
- Audit packs (Step 96) can be produced for a defined period
- Testing and monitoring (Steps 95, 99) occurred on cadence
- Critical failures triggered documented containment and remediation

### What audit readiness cannot prove

- Compliance with every applicable law or regulation (requires legal review)
- Zero losses, zero incidents, or perfect models
- Future performance or absence of unknown failures
- That all operators always followed SOP (sampling may find gaps)
- Broker or market integrity
- Automatic “approved to trade” status for any jurisdiction
