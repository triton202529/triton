# Card 1 — Governance Maturity Philosophy

### Purpose of governance maturity

Governance maturity is **earned institutional capability**—the organization can contain, escalate, evidence, audit, rehearse, and evolve under stress **without** depending on heroes, oral policy, or premature complexity.

### Why governance maturity must be earned rather than rushed

| Rush risk | Earned maturity outcome |
|-----------|---------------------------|
| Manuals before operators | OCR failure; false documentation |
| Committee before quorum discipline | Oral votes; lift disputes |
| Crisis bureaucracy before basics | Process without containment |
| Institutional cert before drills | False confidence (110) |
| Meta-governance before stability | Drift and safeguard dilution |
| Scale before succession | Key-person failure (111) |

### Core principles

| Principle | Maturity meaning |
|-----------|------------------|
| **Capital Preservation Doctrine supremacy** | No stage skips containment |
| **Maturity before authority** | Capability gates (Card 3) |
| **Readiness before complexity** | Lean until evidence supports depth |
| **Institutional patience** | Multi-year timeline (Card 4) |
| **Constitutional safeguards dominate** | Safeguards never traded for speed |
| **Evidence before advancement** | `GOVMAT` requires artifact pack |
| **Resilience before scale** | War games before sovereign claims |
| **Continuity before expansion** | Succession proven before LEAD+ autonomy |

### What governance maturity proves

- Stages were **sequenced** with documented success conditions (Card 4)
- Capabilities **unlocked only** when readiness dependencies met (Card 3)
- Advancement reviews followed **Committee discipline** (Card 6)
- Regression triggers exist and were **honored** (Card 7)
- Long-term evolution is **planned**, not accidental (Card 8)

### What governance maturity cannot guarantee

- Market performance or zero losses
- Permanent “institutional grade” without renewal
- That faster timeline equals safer governance
- Runtime or trading expansion authorization
- Immunity from crisis or personnel turnover

| Stage | Typical Step 94 correlate |

# Card 3 — Maturity Capability Gating Model

Capabilities **unlock by stage + Step 94 level + Step 110 IRS**—never by narrative or tenure alone.

| Capability | Why gated | Readiness dependency | Failure implication | Escalation | Advancement requirement |
|------------|-----------|----------------------|---------------------|------------|-------------------------|
| **Escalation authority (L3+)** | Wrong tier causes harm | Stage OPERATIONAL+; cert L3 track (97) | SLA miss | Lead | Role cert + shadow log |
| **Hard Halt authority** | Capital boundary | Stage GOVERNANCE_DISCIPLINE+; 93 matrix | Unauthorized lift | Committee+Exec | Dual approval trained |
| **Crisis governance** | Systemic stress | Stage RESILIENCE+; 108 exercised | Ad hoc crisis team | Committee+Exec | War game + crisis drill pass |
| **Committee authority** | Votes bind institution | Stage GOVERNANCE_DISCIPLINE+; 106 quorum trained | Oral votes | Chair | Quorum roster; minutes discipline |
| **Audit readiness** | External trust | Stage RESILIENCE+; 107 pack complete | Adverse diligence | Committee 5bd | CLEAN or remediation closed |
| **Certification eligibility (`GOVCERT-INST`)** | False confidence | Stage INSTITUTIONAL+; IRS band met | Runtime overreach | Committee | `GOVREADY-*` + vote |
| **Governance evolution authority (MATERIAL+)** | Safeguard risk | Stage GOVERNANCE_DISCIPLINE+; 98 trained | Drift | Committee | `GOVMETA` evidence |
| **Succession readiness (LEAD+ handoff)** | Key-person risk | Stage INSTITUTIONAL+; 111 drill | Post-transition regression | Committee | `GOVSUCC` closed + monitoring |

**Gate rule:** Lowest unmet dependency **blocks** unlock regardless of executive preference.

# Card 4 — Governance Maturity Timeline

Multi-year progression is **indicative**, not a schedule mandate. Advance only when success conditions met.

```
Foundation → Operational Discipline → Governance Stability
→ Crisis Readiness → Institutional Governance → Sovereign Governance
```

| Phase | Primary objective | Key manuals activated | Success condition | Blocked assumptions | Escalation | Regression risk |
|-------|-------------------|----------------------|-------------------|---------------------|------------|-----------------|
| **Foundation** (Year 0–1) | Incident + GCC habit | 90, 91, 93, 101 | 90d templates + brief compliance | “We’re fine without logs” | Lead daily | Revert to ad hoc |
| **Operational Discipline** (Year 1–2) | Metrics + daily loop | 92, 99, 96, 102 | GHS ≥ WATCH max 20% days | KPIs optional | Executive on Critical | GWS blind |
| **Governance Stability** (Year 2–3) | Authority + change | 93, 98, 94, 103 | Level 3+ 90d; `GOVCHG` clean | Shadow policy | Committee policy | SoD breach |
| **Crisis Readiness** (Year 3–4) | Rehearse failure | 95, 108, 109, 107 | War game pass; audit pack | Crisis = improvise | Committee remediation | Drill fail |
| **Institutional Governance** (Year 4–6) | Certify objectively | 110, 104, 106 | `GOVCERT-INST` + IRS stable | Cert = trade auth | Revoke path | False readiness |
| **Sovereign Governance** (Year 6+) | Generational durability | 111, 112, 113 | Succession + meta-review annual | Maturity permanent | Annual attestation | Complacency |

**Timeline discipline:** Skipping a phase requires **Committee + Executive** waiver with `GOVMAT` dissent recorded and **elevated regression monitoring 90d**.

# Card 5 — Premature Complexity & Governance Overbuilding Model

| Risk | Why dangerous | Failure consequence | Escalation | Corrective expectation |
|------|---------------|---------------------|------------|------------------------|
| **Institutional overbuilding** | Manuals exceed practice | OCR collapse | Lead | Sunset via 112; revert stage |
| **Governance inflation** | New process every incident | Approval queue | Committee | One-in-one-out rule (112) |
| **Process complexity too early** | Operators skip steps | Hidden risk | Lead | Return to OPERATIONAL stage tools |
| **Fake maturity** | Labels without evidence | Diligence fail | Committee | Downgrade Step 94; revoke 110 |
| **Excessive approval chains** | Delayed containment | SLA miss | Executive review | Re-tier Card 3 gates |
| **Premature crisis bureaucracy** | Forms before halts | Slow response | 108 Lead | Crisis lite until RESILIENCE |
| **False institutional confidence** | Quiet market narrative | Unprepared stress | `GOVREADY` withhold | Mandatory war game |

**Lean rule:** New governance artifact at stage *N* requires **demonstrated mastery** of stage *N−1* artifacts for **90 consecutive days**.
