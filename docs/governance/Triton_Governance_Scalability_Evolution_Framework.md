## Scope disclaimer

This framework governs **how Triton governance scales intentionally**—from founder-led operation through multi-team, multi-entity, and multi-jurisdiction growth—without chaotic sprawl, mission dilution, or constitutional erosion.

> **Governance scalability preserves institutional effectiveness through growth — not guaranteed outcomes.**

**Scalability record ID:** `GOVSCALE-YYYY-MM-DD-###` — growth pressure review, scale-state transition, or expansion governance packet; links to `GOVMAT-*` (114), `GOVALIGN-*` (124), `GOVCX-*` (125), `GOVCHG-*` (98).

**Not org implementation:** Headcount plans, legal entity formation, and HR structure are outside this manual; **governance readiness** for growth is in scope.

**Step 114 vs 126:** **114** = maturity **capability gates** and multi-year roadmap stages; **126** = **organizational scale states** and expansion governance discipline.

**Documentation only.** No code, UI, broker, execution, or governance JSON mutation.

# Card 1 — Scalability & Evolution Philosophy

### Purpose of governance scalability

Governance scalability ensures Triton **adds teams, entities, and jurisdictions only when control layer capacity exists**—delegates, Committee throughput, audit packs, training coverage, and mission alignment—not when narrative or AUM pressure outruns institutional discipline.

### Why governance must scale intentionally rather than organically

| Organic growth failure | Intentional scale discipline |
|------------------------|------------------------------|
| Founder oral policy at 50 people | 111 + 121 written precedent |
| New entity without 107 pack | Entity scalability gate |
| Jurisdiction added ad hoc | Jurisdiction playbook Card 5 |
| Committee drowned in growth | 106 + 125 scaling |
| Manuals lag headcount | 114 stage before complexity |
| “We’ll fix governance later” | `GOVSCALE` before expansion |

### Core principles

| Principle | Scalability meaning |
|-----------|---------------------|
| **Capital Preservation Doctrine supremacy** | Never trade safeguards for growth speed |
| **Governance before growth** | Readiness gate before entity/jurisdiction |
| **Structure before expansion** | Delegates, roster, index before scope |
| **Constitutional safeguards dominate** | Same halts at all scales |
| **Clarity before scale** | 113/101 navigable at each state |
| **Stewardship through growth** | 118 fiduciary per domain |
| **Mission continuity** | 124 alignment at each transition |
| **Controlled institutional evolution** | 98 path only |

### What governance scalability proves

- **Twelve domains** assessed per scale transition (Card 2)
- Growth **state** classified (Card 3) with expectations
- Expansion follows **evolution loop** (Card 4) with evidence
- Scaling failures have **playbooks** (Card 5)
- Anti-chaotic-growth controls active (Card 6)

### What governance scalability cannot guarantee

- Successful fundraising or business growth
- Zero friction when scaling
- Identical governance latency at 10× size
- Legal/regulatory approval in new jurisdictions
- That scale alone creates institutional grade
