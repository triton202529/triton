# Card 1 — Governance Codex Philosophy

### Purpose of the Governance Codex

The Codex answers:

> **What is Triton governance as one unified constitutional system?**

It provides **interoperability**: how layers, roles, lifecycles, and manuals connect so operators, executives, auditors, and successors do not navigate a fragmented library under stress.

### Why Triton requires one constitutional source of truth

| Fragmentation risk | Codex outcome |
|--------------------|---------------|
| Correct manual, wrong layer | Hierarchy + system map (Cards 2–3) |
| Role boundary disputes | Interoperability matrix (Card 5) |
| Priority inversion under pressure | Priority order (Card 4) |
| “Which step governs?” | Interpretation guide (Card 8) |
| Audit asks “show the system” | End-to-end model (Card 6) |
| Improvement without stability | Evolution sits below containment (Card 4) |

### Core principles

| Principle | Codex meaning |
|-----------|---------------|
| **Capital Preservation Doctrine supremacy** | Highest priority in Card 4 |
| **Governance before execution** | Observe → escalate before act |
| **Constitutional safeguards dominate** | Step 100 + 93 non-negotiables |
| **Institutional continuity** | Step 111 preserves knowledge |
| **Evidence-first governance** | Every stage in Card 6 requires artifacts |
| **Containment-first governance** | Halts/lock before optimization |
| **Disciplined evolution** | Steps 98 + 112 after stability |
| **Governance interoperability** | Roles and layers explicitly linked |

### What the Codex proves

- Triton governance is a **designed system**, not ad hoc process
- **Authority, escalation, and evidence** have defined homes per layer
- **Cross-manual dependencies** are traceable (Cards 2, 3, 6)
- **Failure modes** have manual-backed prevention (Card 7)
- **Interpretation** is conservative and institutional (Card 8)

### What the Codex cannot replace

| Item | Authoritative home |
|------|-------------------|
| Incident severity, SLAs, halts | **Step 90** |
| Operator session commands | **Steps 91, 102** |
| KPI thresholds, GHS | **Step 92** |
| Approval matrix | **Step 93** |
| Committee votes | **Step 106** |
| Versioned change execution | **Step 98** |
| Supreme principles & conflict supremacy | **Step 100** |
| Situation routing (<10s) | **Step 101** README |

> **Governance evolution improves institutional resilience — not guaranteed outcomes.** (Step 112)

# Card 4 — Governance Priority Order

When principles conflict under pressure, apply **top-down** until resolved through Step 98.

```
Capital Preservation Doctrine
        ↓
Constitutional Safeguards
        ↓
Containment
        ↓
Escalation Discipline
        ↓
Evidence Integrity
        ↓
Governance Stability
        ↓
Institutional Learning
        ↓
Governance Evolution
```

| Priority | Why it exists | Failure implication | Escalation | Dependency |
|----------|---------------|---------------------|------------|------------|
| **Capital Preservation Doctrine** | Core institutional ethic | Resume-for-convenience losses | Immediate L3+ | Step 100 |
| **Constitutional Safeguards** | Non-negotiable controls | CLPR breach, unauthorized mutation | Committee+Exec | 100, 93, 103 |
| **Containment** | Stop harm before optimize | Escalating loss while “fixing” | Hard/Soft Halt per 90 | 91, 102, 108 |
| **Escalation Discipline** | Right owner, SLA | Wrong tier; timeout | 90 chain, 93 | 106 for L4 |
| **Evidence Integrity** | Audit and truth | “It didn’t happen” | Lead; Audit 107 | 96, 107 |
| **Governance Stability** | No thrash during stress | Change during crisis | Defer 98/112 | 99, 110 |
| **Institutional Learning** | Honest improvement | Repeat root cause | `GOVMETA` | 108, 109, 112 Card 8 |
| **Governance Evolution** | Adapt manuals | Drift, bureaucracy | 98 + 112 | Last — never first |

# Card 5 — Role & Authority Interoperability

| Actor | Purpose | Authority boundary | Escalation relationship | What must never happen | Dependencies |
|-------|---------|-------------------|---------------------------|------------------------|--------------|
| **Operators** | Daily GCC posture | Execute playbook; no constitutional lift | → Senior Op → Lead | Enable runtime; self-approve override | 90, 91, 102, 93 |
| **Developers** | Engineering within safeguards | No safeguard change without Lead | → Lead before PR merge | Silent JSON/governance mutation | 103, 93, 98 |
| **Executives** | Oversight & ratification | Ratify; do not operate console | → Committee on constitutional | Pressure bypass of halts | 104, 106, 100 |
| **GCC Operations** | Institutional truth surface | Observe/report; not trading authority | Feeds 99, 96 | Trade from GCC without playbook | 91, 99, 102 |
| **Governance Committee** | Policy votes, Hard lift | Quorum per 106; not daily ops | → Executive constitutional tier | Oral votes; no minutes | 106, 93, 98 |
| **Audit / Review** | Defensibility, retention | Read/evaluate; not approve halts | Adverse → Committee 5bd | Destruction of evidence | 107, 96 |
| **Crisis Governance** | Systemic response | Tier per 108; contain first | Committee+Exec systemic | Ad hoc crisis team | 108, 90, 106 |
| **Readiness Certification** | Objective IRS/cert | Withhold/revoke; not runtime auth | Committee on institutional cert | Sympathy promotion | 110, 94, 92 |
